# Out-of-the-box formats (avoid the "salary gayab / SIP date" tropes every distributor and AMC already uses)
Rule: sell a FEELING and a HABIT, never a product. Pain point first, no fear or shaming, no outcome claims. Hindi must be flowing sentences (full natural sentences, commas, no 2-3 word fragments); numbers written as words in the voice line.
Formats (rotate, never the same twice in a row):
1. FUTURE AAP CHAT (reels_src/reel_chat.py): messages from your 60-year-old self, lock-screen hook, typing dots, a count-up stat card (arithmetic done in code, labelled as an illustration, e.g. inflation at an assumed rate; never fund returns). Spec: reels/team/sample-v3-spec.json.
2. WEDDING-SEASON / FESTIVAL MATH: what a season's gifting and travel does to next quarter's month-end, as arithmetic, no judgement.
3. SHARMA JI KA BETA: the comparison trap, reframed as "compare with your own last year".
4. THE 3 AM WORRY: one worry (parents' medical bill, school fees, job loss) and one calm habit, voiced slowly.
5. MYTH COURT: a courtroom skit, "Aaj ka mukadma: SIP kab shuru karein?", verdict = a principle.
6. PRICE TAG OF A DREAM: a goal with today's price and an assumed-inflation price (illustrative, computed in code).
7. FAMILY GROUP TIP DETOX: why 'pakka tip' forwards are not a plan (no names, no specific stock/fund).
8. DADA vs DADI QUIZ: generations' money habits, ends with a comment question.
Audio: Kokoro Hindi voices are the weak link; for flagship reels prefer a recorded human voice-over (owner or an artist) laid over the same visuals.
