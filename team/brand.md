# DDS brand v2 (replaces the monochrome look)
Colours: Navy #0B1F3A (trust), Cream #FFF6E5 (calm), Turmeric #FFB400 (energy, highlights), Coral #FF5A5F (pain point / myth), Green #00A86B (fix / fact). Reels: Poppins Bold/Medium. Stories: ig_gen templates, then ig_gen.brand(path) (myth: brand(path, top_dark=CORAL)).
Voice: Hinglish on screen (Roman script), spoken by Indian Hindi voices (Kokoro lang hi: hm_omega = Papa/Dada/DDS, hf_alpha = Beta) with the line written in Devanagari in the spec's "voice" field. Never British English.
Tone: a family friend, not a bank. Short sentences. Relatable moments (month-end, family WhatsApp group, festival spending, school fees, EMI, "salary gayab"). Humour allowed; fear, greed, shaming not.
Reel rhythm: hook in the first second, a new visual every 3-5 s, highlight words, one question + "Save / Share / Comment" end card. 25-40 s.
