# Human voice-over mode (owner feedback 28 Nov 2026: computer voices are rejected; wants a real, fast, confident creator voice)
Record ONE take on a phone (quiet room, mic close, energetic, conversational Hinglish), reading the spec's lines in order with a clear 1 second pause between lines. Put the file in the repo (e.g. reels/team/voice/YYYY-MM-DD.m4a) and set "voice_file" in the spec. reels_src/reel_chat.py then cuts the take into lines at the pauses and times every bubble/scene to it. If the number of spoken parts does not match the script it stops and says so.
Reference creators: copy the DELIVERY (fast, direct, a clear promise in the first sentence, numbered list, one question at the end, "Save this"), never the CONTENT. DDS must not name schemes/AMCs, call anything 'best' or 'top', mention returns, or compare direct vs regular plans: AMFI/SEBI distributor rules (see compliance.md).
Compliant list-reel ideas in that style: "5 questions to ask before you start a SIP", "5 SIP mistakes I see families make", "5 lines to check on any factsheet", "5 things your SIP date can't fix".
